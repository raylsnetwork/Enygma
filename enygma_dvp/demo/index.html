What you're about to see ▾

A Delivery-vs-Payment (DvP) atomic swap: Alice deposits 50 USDT into a private vault, Bob deposits an NFT ticket into a separate vault, and they swap — Alice ends up with the ticket, Bob ends up with the USDT — with no counterparty risk: either both legs settle on-chain, or neither does.

LEG 1 Alice runs the DvP Initiator circuit, locking in the swap terms and encrypting an offer for Bob with his ML-KEM-768 view key.
LEG 2 Bob decrypts the offer, runs the DvP Destination circuit to accept it, and submits — settling both sides atomically in one transaction.
