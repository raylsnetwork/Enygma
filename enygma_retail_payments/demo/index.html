What you're about to see ▾

Alice deposits ERC-20 tokens into a private vault behind a Poseidon commitment, then pays Bob 30 of 40 tokens using a Groth16 zero-knowledge proof — submitted by a relayer so Alice's Ethereum address never touches the payment transaction. Bob discovers and decrypts his note using an ML-KEM-768 (post-quantum) view key.

PLAIN Bob finds his note by trial-decrypting every on-chain Payment event with his view key.
WITH TAG Alice also opens an ML-KEM channel and publishes an encrypted "tag" pointer, so Bob can find his note directly without scanning every event.
